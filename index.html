Here They Comes: Zombie Siege

Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 · iPhone, iPad and Mac

Here They Comes: Zombie Siege is an offline game published by Joseph Bryan Osias Baterna. You can play without creating an account. We do not collect or receive personal information from gameplay, track you across apps or websites, or sell your information. The game contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

What stays on your device

The game saves your map seed, settlement, resources, construction progress, survival statistics and horde schedule so you can resume your run. It also remembers preferences such as sound levels and graphics settings. Manual saves, autosaves and recovery copies are stored in the game’s local data folder. The developer cannot access these files remotely.

Removing saves and settings

On iPhone or iPad, choose Delete App to remove the game and its local data. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. On Mac, quit the game and remove its saved data from the game’s container in your user Library. Email us if you need help locating the folder. Removing local data permanently erases those settlements from the device.

If you back up your device, a backup may retain an earlier copy. You control those copies through your backup settings.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads and may process diagnostics under your Apple privacy settings. If you enable sharing with app developers or send feedback through TestFlight, Apple may make a report available to us. We use reports we receive to investigate problems with the game.
